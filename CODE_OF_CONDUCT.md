# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making this project welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect the decisions of project maintainers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Threats or any other conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe.
- Any behavior that is unlawful or inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the contact information listed in the repository or npm package metadata. Please include enough detail to understand what happened and any relevant links or evidence.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project or its community.
